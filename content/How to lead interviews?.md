First, ask if it's still a good time for the candidate to do the interview.  
It's rare, but they might not be in a good place.  
The option to reschedule might be really appreciated.  

Apologies in advance because you are going to interrupt them.  
Explain that it's to make sure you can go through everything.  

Apologies in advance because you might not reply.
Explain that it's because you are taking notes.  

Be friendly.  
Your goal is to assess them, but also to make them want to work with you.  

Be even more friendly if the candidate is failing.  
If they are and you know they are failing, remove your interviewer hat and put your teacher hat.  

If they ask how they performed during the interview, tell them you can't give them feedback at this stage of the process.
