---
title: How to write tech doc?
draft: false
---
Use simple words.  
Write small sentences.  

Don't use acronyms.  

Use numbers:   
"A lot of errors" -> "42 errors"  
"A small amount of users" -> "5 users"  

Lose useless adjectives:  
"A very important message" -> "An important message"  

Order sections by most important first.  

Keep it short.  
Add as many appendices you want.  
Discuss trade-offs in appendices.
